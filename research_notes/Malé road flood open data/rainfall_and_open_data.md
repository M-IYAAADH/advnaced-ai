# Open sub-daily rainfall for Malé and open GIS layers for Malé island proper (as of 15 Nov 2026)

Method note: the items marked "verified (live check 15 Nov 2026)" were tested against live APIs or downloads in this session: the NASA CMR granule search, the HDX CKAN API, the geoBoundaries API, Ogimet getsynop and the Meteostat bulk files. Items marked "not verified here" come from documentation or prior knowledge only and need checking before they are cited. No data were saved into the project. One COD-AB xlsx went to the scratchpad for inspection only.

## (A1) GPM IMERG: access route, versions and the period actually available

### Takeaway
IMERG V07 half-hourly data at 0.1° covers the whole Dec 2021 to Oct 2026 window only through the **Late** (~14 h latency) and **Early** (~4 h) runs. As of 15 Nov 2026, the research-grade **Final** run ends on **30 Sep 2025**. The Early and Late runs also change algorithm version mid-period, from **V07B to V07C during March 2026**. A homogeneous Final-run series for Dec 2021 to Oct 2026 therefore does not exist yet.

### Cited Findings
- The Final half-hourly product is "GPM IMERG Final Precipitation L3 Half Hourly 0.1 degree x 0.1 degree V07 (GPM_3IMERGHH)". It is published by NASA GES DISC, has DOI 10.5067/GPM/IMERG/3B-HH/07, uses a 0.1° x 0.1° (about 10 km) half-hourly grid and is global. Its stated latency is "~4 months after the observation month". The licence in the metadata is US Government Works (public domain). Access routes are the GES DISC page https://disc.gsfc.nasa.gov/datacollection/GPM_3IMERGHH_07.html, Earthdata Search (collection C2723754847-GES_DISC), the CMR virtual directory and Giovanni (https://giovanni.gsfc.nasa.gov/#dataKeyword=IMERGHH). The page says V07B is current and older versions are superseded — [data.nasa.gov record](https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/gpm-imerg-final-precipitation-l3-half-hourly-0-1-degree-x-0-1-degree-v07-gpm-3imerghh-at-g-fb698)
- **Verified (live check 15 Nov 2026):** the CMR collection metadata for GPM_3IMERGHH v07 gives time_start 1998-01-01 and **time_end 2025-09-30T23:59:59Z**. The latest granule is `3B-HHR.MS.MRG.3IMERG.20250930-S233000-E235959.1410.V07B.HDF5`. The Final run therefore lags by about 13 months, not the advertised ~4 — [CMR granule search](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/granules.json?short_name=GPM_3IMERGHH&version=07&sort_key=-start_date&page_size=1). The cause of the delay was not found.
- **Verified (live check):** the Late half-hourly collection is GPM_3IMERGHHL (C2723754845-GES_DISC). Its latest granule is 2026-11-14T23:30Z, and it has granules for 2021-12-01 (V07B). The Early half-hourly collection is GPM_3IMERGHHE (C2723758340-GES_DISC). Its latest granule is 2026-11-15T09:30Z, and it also has 2021-12-01 granules — [CMR Late](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/granules.json?short_name=GPM_3IMERGHHL&version=07&sort_key=-start_date&page_size=1); [CMR Early](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/granules.json?short_name=GPM_3IMERGHHE&version=07&sort_key=-start_date&page_size=1)
- **Verified (live check):** Late-run granule file names show **V07B through at least 2026-03-01 and V07C from 2026-03-10 onward**, with all samples after that date V07C. The exact switch date lies between 1 and 10 March 2026. No release note for V07C was found — [CMR granule search](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/granules.json?short_name=GPM_3IMERGHHL&version=07&temporal=2026-03-10T00:00:00Z,2026-03-10T00:10:00Z)
- V07 Early and Late production began at PPS on 1 June 2024. Retrospective V07 processing for Early, Late and Final was complete by Aug 2024. A second Final reprocessing is labelled V07B — [IMERG V07 release notes (Nov 2024)](https://gpm.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/2024-12/IMERG_V07_ReleaseNotes_241126.pdf)
- NASA advises using the Final run for research unless latency requires Early or Late — [GPM: IMERG Final Run](https://gpm.nasa.gov/taxonomy/term/1417)
- Registration: GES DISC downloads need a free NASA Earthdata Login account. The Earthdata Search and CMR routes above are part of that system — [data.nasa.gov record](https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/gpm-imerg-final-precipitation-l3-half-hourly-0-1-degree-x-0-1-degree-v07-gpm-3imerghh-at-g-fb698)
- Google Earth Engine also hosts IMERG half-hourly data (catalogue ID believed to be `NASA/GPM_L3/IMERG_V07`). **Not verified here.** The ID and whether it mixes Early, Late and Final need checking in the GEE catalogue.

### Inferences
- For Dec 2021 to Oct 2026 the practical choice is either (i) the **Late run** for the whole period, keeping a `version` column for the V07B to V07C change, or (ii) the **Final run** to Sep 2025 plus Late from Oct 2025, flagged as a change of product. Option (i) is more homogeneous. Option (ii) is better quality for the earlier events. Either choice needs to be documented.
- Malé island proper (about 2 km²) is much smaller than one 0.1° cell (about 120 km²). IMERG values are cell averages over mostly ocean, not rainfall at a street.
- Half-hourly IMERG files store a rate in mm/hr. Accumulating to 1 h, 3 h, 6 h and 24 h means multiplying each value by 0.5 h and keeping the UTC time stamps. Malé is UTC+5. This is standard IMERG practice but was not re-read in the documentation here.

### Gaps
- Why the Final run stops at Sep 2025, and when it will catch up.
- The V07C change log, and whether Early and Late V07C differ systematically from V07B.
- The GEE asset ID and its update status were not checked.

## (A2) Other sub-daily rainfall options

### Takeaway
The best open **gauge** source for sub-daily rain at Velana/Hulhulé (WMO 43555) is the raw SYNOP archive on **Ogimet**. It runs from Dec 2021 to Oct 2026 and gives 6-h and 12-h accumulations plus a 24-h total, with no registration. Meteostat redistributes this station but its bulk feed stops in March 2026. GSMaP (hourly, 0.1°, free registration) is the main satellite alternative to IMERG. ERA5 is reanalysis, not observation. CHIRPS is daily only.

### Cited Findings
- **Ogimet SYNOP, station 43555 (Velana/Hulhulé). Verified (live check 15 Nov 2026).** Plain-text CSV-like lines of raw FM-12 SYNOP come back with no login from `https://www.ogimet.com/cgi-bin/getsynop?block=43555&begin=YYYYMMDDHHmm&end=YYYYMMDDHHmm`. Live samples returned data for 1 Dec 2021, 15 Jan 2022, 1 May 2024 and 1 Oct 2026, at 3-hourly reports (00, 03, 06, 09 and 12 UTC seen). Rain is coded in Section 1 group `6RRRtR`. In the samples, the 00Z report `60022` decodes as 2 mm over 12 h, and 06Z `60001` decodes as 0 mm over 6 h. The Section 3 `7RRRR` group gives a 24-h total; `70078` decodes as 7.8 mm. Some reports are NIL (missing), for example 03Z on 1 May 2024 — [Ogimet getsynop sample](https://www.ogimet.com/cgi-bin/getsynop?block=43555&begin=202405010000&end=202405011200). These decodings use standard WMO FM-12 coding. **An hourly rain group was not seen**, so SYNOP gives 6, 12 and 24-hour sums, not 1-hour sums. Ogimet's terms of use and rate limits were not checked.
- **Meteostat. Verified (live check).** Station 43555 "Male" (ICAO VRMM, 4.2N 73.5333E) appears in the bulk station list. Its inventory shows hourly data 1981-01-01 to **2026-03-10** and model data to 2026-03-20. In the bulk hourly CSVs (`https://bulk.meteostat.net/v2/hourly/{year}/43555.csv.gz`), non-empty precipitation counts were: 2021, 0 of 8,760 rows; 2022, 5,243; 2023, 8,760; 2024, 8,523; 2025, 8,457 of 8,760; 2026, 1,798 of 2,113 rows (to about March) — [Meteostat bulk station list](https://bulk.meteostat.net/v2/stations/lite.json.gz). The fully populated 2023 field is suspicious. Meteostat's hourly `prcp` for a SYNOP station that reports 6-hourly may be redistributed or model-filled. **Do not treat it as measured hourly rain** until Meteostat's method is checked. Other Maldives stations listed are 43533 Hanimaadhoo, 43577 Kadhdhoo, 43588 Thinadhoo, 43599 Gan, VRMG0 and VRMH0. None is on Malé island proper.
- **GSMaP (JAXA).** It covers rainfall data from March 2000 at 0.1° and hourly. GSMaP_NOW is updated every 30 minutes, with global coverage since June 2019. Products include NRT, MVK (standard), Gauge-adjusted (GSMaP_Gauge, Gauge_NRT, Gauge_RNL) and NOW. Download is by password-protected FTP after free registration. The terms require acknowledging JAXA, and JAXA asks to be contacted before wide redistribution — [JAXA GSMaP guide](https://sharaku.eorc.jaxa.jp/GSMaP/guide.html). The current algorithm version, the latency of each product and the exact periods were not confirmed on the page fetched. The page refers to "GPM-GSMaP Ver.6" in one place, which may be outdated.
- **ERA5 / ERA5-Land (Copernicus CDS).** The ERA5 single-levels catalogue page responded (HTTP 200) at https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels. **Details not verified here (prior knowledge):** ERA5 is hourly at 0.25°, from 1940 to near-present, with about 5 days' latency (preliminary ERA5T). ERA5-Land is hourly at 0.1°. Both are under the CC-BY 4.0 licence and need a free ECMWF/CDS account and the `cdsapi` API. ERA5-Land is masked to land. Malé is far smaller than its grid cell, so the nearest cell may be ocean or missing. Reanalysis precipitation is model output, not observation.
- **PERSIANN-CCS (UC Irvine CHRS).** **Not verified here.** It is understood to be hourly at about 0.04°, near-real-time, and distributed through the CHRS Data Portal. Confirm the URL, period and terms before citing.
- **CHIRPS.** Daily is its finest step (as stated in the brief), so it is not sub-daily. Separately, HDX holds a WFP "Maldives: Rainfall Indicators at Subnational Level" dataset. It is CHIRPS-based, **dekadal (10-day)**, covers 1981-01-01 to 2026-11-10, carries a CC BY licence and is updated every 14 days — [HDX mdv-rainfall-subnational](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/mdv-rainfall-subnational). It is too coarse in time for flood events.
- **Maldives Meteorological Service (meteorology.gov.mv).** The site responds and publishes downloadable monthly climate reports with station monthly rainfall totals. One example is Hanimaadhoo, May 2022: 511 mm, against 341 mm in May 2021. It also publishes monthly rainfall outlooks. No public hourly or daily data download and no published data-request procedure were found. The Climate Section is at Hulhulé — [MMS downloads, for example](https://meteorology.gov.mv/downloads/199/view). Whether the monthly reports give daily maxima for Malé or Hulhulé was not checked.
- **Weather Underground personal stations in Malé.** Not researched or verified in this session. Such stations are typically uncalibrated, and their history may need an API key under restrictive terms.

### Inferences
- A defensible event-rainfall table for Dec 2021 to Oct 2026 could use (1) Ogimet SYNOP 43555 for gauge 6-h, 12-h and 24-h totals, decoded with QC and NIL handling, and (2) IMERG Late or Final half-hourly for the cell containing Malé to get the intensity profile. Both are at or over Hulhulé and the surrounding sea, not on Malé island proper, and that should be stated.
- The project's NOAA ISD/GSOD files come from the same 43555/VRMM SYNOP and METAR stream. Ogimet mainly fills the period after Aug 2025 and lets the raw decoding be checked.

### Gaps
- No open **hourly or sub-hourly gauge rainfall on Malé island proper** was found. MMS data would need a request.
- No licence or terms were checked for Ogimet.
- Meteostat's precipitation method for 43555, and why the bulk feed stops in March 2026, were not established.

## (A3) How well satellite products capture heavy rain on small islands

### Takeaway
No IMERG or GSMaP validation study specific to the Maldives was found. The nearest evidence comes from the Maritime Continent: IMERG does reasonably for fairly extreme daily rain (around the 85th–95th percentile, or the top 5%) but **overestimates the most extreme (top 1%) daily totals**. Errors also vary across the land–coast–ocean transition, and a single point gauge poorly represents a 0.1° cell.

### Cited Findings
- **IMERG (V06B) against gauges in peninsular Malaysia and the Philippines:** it is described as reliable for fairly extreme rainfall (the top 5% of daily totals) but tends to overestimate very extreme rainfall (the top 1%). The authors say earlier studies may have underestimated IMERG skill because gauge spatial sampling error is large during extremes — [Reading CentAUR record](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/id/eprint/101615); [UEA record](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/80260). These findings were taken from search summaries. The full text was not read, and the summary wording should be checked before quoting.
- **US coastal study:** IMERG errors vary across the land–coast–ocean continuum and relate to its passive-microwave, infrared and morphing parts. Evaluation in "nontraditional" regions is called a priority — [NOAA repository PDF](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/47949/noaa_47949_DS1.pdf). This also comes from a search summary and was not read in full.

### Inferences
- Short convective bursts over a 2 km² island can be smoothed out by a 10 km, 30-minute product, or put in the wrong place or time. Peak intensities from IMERG will probably differ from a street-level gauge. Satellite rainfall is best treated as an event covariate with stated uncertainty, not as the rainfall that fell on a road.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed validation for the Maldives or Indian Ocean atolls was found in this session. A further search (for example "IMERG Indian Ocean atoll gauge", "GSMaP Maldives") is recommended.

## (B) Open GIS layers for Malé island proper (data.gov.mv, MBS, HDX, LSA, geoBoundaries, OSM)

### Takeaway
Openly downloadable layers that resolve Malé island proper are the OSM-derived roads, buildings and waterways, the COD-AB boundaries and the modelled population grids. **No open ward, census-block, land-parcel or drainage GIS layer for Malé was verified.** The MBS publishes Census 2022 "Block Maps", but no GIS download was found. data.gov.mv could not be checked automatically because it sits behind a Cloudflare challenge.

### Cited Findings
- **HDX COD-AB "Maldives - Subnational Administrative Boundaries" (cod-ab-mdv).** Published by OCHA, with the Government of Maldives as source. Version v02 has Admin 1 (21 units) and Admin 2 (1,556 units, i.e. islands). It is valid from 22 Oct 2024 and was reviewed on 30 Oct 2025. The licence is CC BY-IGO, and it comes as GDB, SHP, GeoJSON or XLSX. **Verified (live check):** the XLSX contains the name "Male", but no ward names (Henveiru, Galolhu, Maafannu, Machchangoalhi) were found in it. The finest unit that includes Malé is therefore the island or city, not the ward — [HDX cod-ab-mdv](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-mdv); [SHP download](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/a968d227-24c9-49ea-bbce-cee04ee94819/resource/21c520dd-b1a0-4e76-af6c-11dff697401d/download/mdv_admin_boundaries.shp.zip)
- **HDX COD-PS (cod-ps-mdv), UNFPA with the Maldives National Bureau of Statistics as source.** 2022 sex- and age-disaggregated population at admin 0–2, CC BY-IGO, in XLS, XLSX and CSV — [HDX cod-ps-mdv](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ps-mdv)
- **HDX 2014 Census admin 1–3 population (CC0).** National Bureau of Statistics and NDMC, cleaned by the Maldives National University, as CSV tables with no geometry — [HDX 2014 census](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/maldives-2014-population-and-housing-census-administrative-level-1-to-3). Whether admin 3 corresponds to Malé wards was not checked.
- **geoBoundaries (gbOpen MDV). Verified (API).** ADM0 (2021, from Sentinel-2 land cover, CC BY 4.0), ADM1 (2017, OSM/Wambacher, ODbL, 21 units) and ADM2 (2017, Government of Maldives / OCHA ROAP, CC BY 3.0 IGO, 21 units). Nothing is finer than atoll or city — [geoBoundaries API](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/MDV/ALL/)
- **HOT/OSM country exports on HDX (ODbL; refreshed every 30 days; last snapshot 3 Nov 2026):** roads (`highway=*`), buildings (`building=*`) and waterways, in GPKG, SHP, GeoParquet, PMTiles and GeoJSON, each with a data-quality report. Example: https://production-raw-data-api.s3.amazonaws.com/ISO3/MDV/roads/hotosm_mdv_roads_osm_gpkg.zip — [HDX hotosm_mdv_roads](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/hotosm_mdv_roads); [HDX hotosm_mdv_buildings](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/hotosm_mdv_buildings); [HDX hotosm_mdv_waterways](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/hotosm_mdv_waterways). There are also POI, populated-places and health and education facility layers.
- **Other HDX Maldives layers that may help (verified as listed in the API):** "Maldives: Road Surface Data" and "Maldives: Planet Road Surface, Width, and Passability Data" (HeiGIT), "Maldives - Critical infrastructures" (OCHA ROAP, 2024), "Maldives - Disaster Records" (OCHA ROAP, 2024), "Kontur boundaries" (ODbL, 2022/2023 GPKG), "Kontur population 400 m H3", "Meta High Resolution Population Density Maps", WorldPop population counts and density, Copernicus GHSL, and "AI-Detected Missing Roads" (Meta) — [HDX CKAN API search](https://data.humdata.org/api/3/action/package_search?q=maldives&rows=120). Their licences and island-level resolution were not checked one by one, except where stated above.
- **Maldives Bureau of Statistics.** The Census 2022 page defines "Maale" as the four wards Henveiru, Galolhu, Machchangoalhi and Maafannu, plus Hulhumaale, Villimaale and K. Hulhule with its harbour. Results come as XLS and PDF tables (P3–P5, population by place of enumeration) — [MBS Census 2022](https://statisticsmaldives.gov.mv/census-2022/). A "Block Maps of Maldives" page, published 17 Dec 2024, lists one entry per atoll including "Male'". **Verified (live check):** no PDF, SHP, KML or ZIP links appear in the static HTML, so the format and whether they can be downloaded are unconfirmed — [MBS Block Maps](https://statisticsmaldives.gov.mv/block-maps-of-maldives/)
- **data.gov.mv.** DNS resolves to Cloudflare. Automated requests got HTTP 403 and a "Just a moment..." challenge page, so its catalogue could not be listed. The site should be checked manually in a browser.
- **Maldives Land and Survey Authority / a national geoportal.** `geoportal.gov.mv` and `lsa.gov.mv` do not resolve (NXDOMAIN). No open LSA download was found. The project already holds an official OneMap Malé polygon (see CLAUDE.md), which suggests OneMap is the government geoportal route; its terms were not rechecked here.
- **Drainage.** No open GIS layer of Malé drains, inlets or outfalls was found on HDX, geoBoundaries or OSM-derived exports. The OSM "waterways" export is unlikely to contain urban storm drains.

### Inferences
- For a road-susceptibility map, an open base would be: OSM roads (HOT export or a direct OSM extract), COD-AB or OneMap island boundary, building sources kept separate, and modelled population (WorldPop, Meta or Kontur) for exposure only. Ward-level aggregation would need ward polygons digitised from MBS block maps or requested from MBS, the City Council or LSA.
- Census population exists at island or city level. Malé city in the census includes Hulhumalé and Vilimalé, so census totals for "Malé" must not be applied to Malé island proper without separating them.

### Gaps
- What data.gov.mv holds (blocked by Cloudflare).
- The format and licence of the MBS Malé block maps.
- Any open drainage, land-parcel or ward GIS for Malé. These are probably held by agencies (LSA, Malé City Council, the stormwater project custodian) and need a request.
