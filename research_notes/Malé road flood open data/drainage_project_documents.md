Scope: Malé island proper. Status labels used below:
- **VERIFIED-OPENED**: URL opened in this session and content read (text or PDF extracted).
- **SEARCH-ONLY**: appeared in search results; not opened, contents unconfirmed.
- **NOT FOUND**: searched, no public copy located.

Bottom line: no public document located in this session contains a drain-alignment map, pump-station location list, outfall list, survey levels or a named list of flood-prone Malé streets. The public material is project-level text: problem descriptions, procurement records, cancellation records and news. ## World Bank Maldives Urban Development and Resilience Project (MUDR, P163957): what do the public documents say about the Malé stormwater design consultancy and the masterplan/DEM EOI?

### Takeaway
MUDR funded one Malé stormwater design consultancy, MV-MONPI-234588-CS-CQS. It was signed for about US$320k, and it was meant to produce designs and tender documents. The masterplan + DEM consultancy, MV-MONPI-326937-CS-QCBS, the US$0.63M stormwater master plan and the US$5.17M Malé stormwater civil works were all cancelled in 2024 after the government changed policy direction. No design report, drawing, drain map or DEM from these contracts was found in public. The PAD gives only aggregate system facts, such as 15 pump stations built in 2014–2018, gutters at 6 m intervals and average flood depth of about 0.4 m in the reclaimed south and southwest. It names no streets.

### Cited Findings
**Project Appraisal Document (PAD), Report No. PAD2923, World Bank, 31 Jan 2020, VERIFIED-OPENED (text version, first ~100k of 222k characters read)**: [PDF](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/753601583118045862/pdf/Maldives-Urban-Development-Resilience-Project.pdf); [TXT](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/753601583118045862/txt/Maldives-Urban-Development-Resilience-Project.txt)
- The bulk of Malé's drainage system is "over forty years old and has not been functional over the past decade". [PAD2923](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/753601583118045862/txt/Maldives-Urban-Development-Resilience-Project.txt)
- The system is described as gutters on paved roads at 6 m intervals. They fail because of cementation between paving blocks, lack of maintenance and heavier rainfall. Clogged grids and gutters and broken pipes are common. [PAD2923](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/753601583118045862/txt/Maldives-Urban-Development-Resilience-Project.txt)
- 15 pumping stations were installed in 2014–2018, with pump collection chambers, and street gutters in reclaimed neighbourhoods were connected. Locations and capacities are not given in the portion read. [PAD2923](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/753601583118045862/txt/Maldives-Urban-Development-Resilience-Project.txt)
- Malé floods several times a year, with average flood depths of about 0.4 m on the reclaimed south and southwest side. The December 2018 event brought about half the yearly average rainfall in six hours. No individual streets are named. ### Inferences
- The design consultancy MV-MONPI-234588 was contracted in 2022 and most likely delivered designs and tender documents for Malé. The ISR prose says "delivered", while its indicator still read "No" in Oct 2024. Only the underlying drawings would contain drain alignments, inlets, pump sumps or levels, and they were **not** found in any public World Bank, MoF or audit document. They would have to be requested from the custodian, the Ministry of Construction, Housing and Infrastructure PMU (formerly MNPHI), possibly with MWSC.
- On the DEM: the Feb 2024 procurement plan shows MV-MONPI-326937 "Under Implementation" with a final scheduled date of 2024-08-13. The ISR and audit then show the US$0.63M master plan dropped in 2024. That supports the working-file claim that the masterplan/DEM process was cancelled around August 2024 and that no DEM was delivered. **The exact date of 12 August 2024 was not confirmed from a primary source in this session.** The 2024-08-13 figure is a scheduled procurement date, not a cancellation notice.
- The local `Data_Request_Specification.md` says the detailed designs were "reported completed May 2024 by the World Bank". This session did not locate that specific May 2024 statement. ISR Seq. 10 supports "delivered" in prose but contradicts it in the indicator.

### Gaps
- PAD Annex 5 (Maps) and the remaining ~122k characters of the PAD text were not read. The annex may hold a Malé location map, but it is unlikely to show drains.
- The final World Bank documents (latest ISR, Implementation Completion Report after the June 2026 closing date) were not found or checked.
- No ESMF, ESIA or ESMP for the Malé stormwater works was located. The PID only says a separate EIA was planned.
- No public design report, drawings, GIS/CAD or survey data from MV-MONPI-234588 was found.

## ADB, JICA, AFD, Saudi Fund or other donor projects on Malé drainage

### Takeaway
The only active donor project for Malé island drainage found is Japan's grant "Project for Disaster Resilience Enhancement in Malé Island (Construction Phase)". It was signed on 9 Sep 2025 for JPY 1.56 billion (about US$10.5M) and covers seawall repair and stormwater drainage equipment. Package 2, about US$1.5M and signed Feb 2026, automates **16** Malé pump stations and adds a monitoring station and a jetting vehicle. No JICA preparatory survey report with maps was found. No ADB, AFD or Saudi Fund Malé-island drainage project was found.

### Cited Findings
- Government of Maldives and JICA grant agreement, "Project for Disaster Resilience Enhancement in Malé Island (Construction Phase)": signed 9 Sep 2025 (article dated 10 Sep 2025), JPY 1.56 billion (about US$10.5M), to "provide stormwater drainage equipment to mitigate risks from waves and heavy rainfall". No locations beyond Malé are named and no survey report is cited. VERIFIED-OPENED. [MFR.mv](https://mfr.mv/public-sector/government-of-maldives-and-jica-have-signed-an-agreement-for-the-project-for-disaster-resilience-enhancement-in-male)
- Package 1, seawall repair for north and east Malé "including installation of rainwater drainage equipment": contract with Toyo Construction Co., Ltd., 487 days, about JPY 700M (about MVR 71M). SEARCH-ONLY. [Atoll Times](https://atolltimes.mv/post/news/14952); [Corporate Maldives](https://corporatemaldives.com/jica-grant-to-fund-seawall-upgrades-and-drainage-systems-in-male/)
- Package 2, dated 23 Feb 2026: "16 pump stations in Malé will be fully automated", with a new monitoring station for round-the-clock monitoring of the pump network and a jetting vehicle for drain maintenance. A Japanese engineering firm (not named) does the work for about US$1.5M within 487 days, under Japan's Economic and Social Development Programme. Pump locations and capacities are not given. VERIFIED-OPENED. [archive.mv](https://archive.mv/en/articles/4ZkW8A)
- A search of openjicareport.jica.go.jp returned no Malé preparatory survey. All hits were for other countries. [JICA report search hit, unrelated](https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12270823_01.pdf)
- ADB Greater Malé Environmental Improvement and Waste Management Project (51077) covers solid waste, not stormwater, so it is context only. [ADB 51077-002](https://www.adb.org/projects/51077-002/main)

### Inferences
- The pump-station count differs between sources: 15 built in 2014–2018 according to the PAD, and 16 to be automated according to the 2026 news. This could reflect a station added later or different counting. A JICA preparatory survey for the Malé island project probably exists, since JICA grant aid normally has one, and may contain pump-station locations. It was not found publicly.

### Gaps
- No JICA preparatory survey or outline design report was located. Next step: search openjicareport.jica.go.jp by country "Maldives" for 2023–2025 reports.
- AFD, Saudi Fund and Indian-assistance Malé-island stormwater projects were not specifically searched or found, so this is a weak negative.

## MWSC or government announcements naming Malé pump stations, locations, capacities or roads drained

### Takeaway
Public announcements give counts and some deployment locations but no complete inventory of pump stations or their capacities. The named locations are temporary council pump deployments: the IGMH area, West Park and the Maafannu ferry terminal. They are not fixed stations.

### Cited Findings
- On 2 Mar 2025 Malé City Council deployed high-capacity pumps at **10 locations**, naming the **IGMH area, West Park, and the Maafannu ferry terminal**. The new pumps move 11,356 L/min; earlier pumps moved 1,300 and 900 L/min. The headline's "11,000 tonnes per minute" is a unit error. The council said the drainage system "poses challenges", so flood control needs several agencies. VERIFIED-OPENED. [Edition.mv](https://edition.mv/news/39788)
- On 12 Dec 2021 (per the search summary) MWSC said the system is designed to pump out flood water within 2–3 hours of continuous heavy rain, and its MD blamed inadequate design and undersized pumps. SEARCH-ONLY. [Times of Addu](https://timesofaddu.com/2021/12/12/capital-city-male-floods-with-heavy-monsoon-rains/)
- MWSC said Malé's stormwater system capacity would be doubled, with talks on quadrupling pump capacity. The article date is unknown and the page returned HTTP 403 when fetched. [PSM News](https://psmnews.mv/en/131627)
- The then Housing Minister, Mohamed Muizzu, blamed sabotage of pump-station panel boards for one flood. The date is unclear from the snippet. SEARCH-ONLY. [Maldives Independent](https://maldivesindependent.com/society/minister-blames-sabotage-of-pump-stations-for-flooding-142341)
- In Jan 2025 MNDF reported 30,569 tonnes of water drained from Malé roads over 21 hours. SEARCH-ONLY. [see.mv](https://see.mv/94265)

### Inferences
- These are positive-only, news-derived location mentions, consistent with the `male-flood-basis/` compilation. Pump deployment sites show where agencies chose to pump. They are not verified flooded-road labels, and they cannot establish that other roads stayed dry.

### Gaps
- No MWSC page listing pump-station locations or capacities was found. mwsc.com.mv was not searched directly. Pump-station locations would also need to be checked against OSM (`man_made=pumping_station`) or requested from MWSC or the ministry.
- No public survey levels, invert levels or outfall locations for Malé were found in any source.
