MNDF has undertaken work of dewatering the flooded roads of Male City

Updated: 17-07-2022, 20:50


Firefighters of MNDF Fire and Rescue Service have undertaken work of dewatering the flooded roads of Male’ City.

Firefighters of FRS have begun work of dewatering the roads near Ekuveni running track and IGMH area, flooded by heavy rain.
