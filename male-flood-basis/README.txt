Greater Malé rain-flood basis (compiled 14 Nov 2026)

flood_map.html
  The flood map and write-up. Open in a browser. Needs an internet
  connection the first time to load the map library and fonts.

data/male_daily_features_with_flood_labels.csv
  One row per day, 2020 to 13 Nov 2026: flood label plus main predictors. Start here.
data/flood_incidents_greater_male_2021-2026.csv
  The 18 incidents with dates, reported rainfall, places and source links.
data/flood_locations_greater_male.geojson
  Highlighted roads and markers with counts (QGIS or code).
data/male_reanalysis_hourly_2020-2026.csv, male_reanalysis_daily_2020-2026.csv
  ERA5 reanalysis via Open-Meteo. Underestimates peak rainfall.
data/hulhule_station_daily_2020-2025.csv
  NOAA GSOD station observations. Rainfall column incomplete; ends 24 Aug 2025.
data/male_sea_level_waves_hourly_2022-2026.csv
  Modelled sea level (with tide), waves and swell from 23 Nov 2022.
data/male_forecast_hourly_snapshot_2026-11-14.csv
  MET Norway forecast snapshot.
data/flooded_roads_and_places_greater_male.csv
  One row per flooded road, landmark or area: times reported, incident dates,
  coordinates (road midpoint) and source links. Roads are listed first.
