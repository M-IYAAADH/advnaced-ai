## Letter 1: Malé City Council

**Subject:** Request for flood report records for a student research project on road flooding in Malé

Dear [Name or title, e.g. Director of Disaster Management],

We are three undergraduate students at Villa College, studying for a Bachelor (Hons) in Computer Science under Ms. Tamilarasi Rajamani. Our project asks a practical question: which roads and road sections on Malé island proper are most prone to flooding after heavy rain?

So far we have mapped the roads named in news reports of rain flooding between December 2021 and October 2026. News coverage favours main roads, and it never tells us which streets stayed clear. Council records would give a far more accurate picture.

We would be grateful for any of the following that the Council can share:

1. **Flood reports or complaints for Malé island proper**, for the dates listed below or for any period from 2021 to 2026. For each, the date and time (with time zone), the road or block, and whether flooding was seen on inspection. **Places that were checked and found clear** are especially useful to us.
2. **The event register and flood hazard maps** that support the Draft Malé City Disaster Management Plan 2025, if these can be shared.
3. **Drainage network maps** (drains, inlets, outfalls, pump stations), or the name of the office that holds them.

Dates from news reports: 11 Dec 2021, 17 Jul 2022, 1 Apr 2023, 21 Jul 2023, 30–31 Dec 2023, 10 Jan 2024, 12 Jan 2024, 13 Aug 2024, 20–21 Dec 2024, 2–3 Jan 2025, 11 Jan 2025, 1 Mar 2025, 11–12 Mar 2025, 25 Nov 2025, 16 Apr 2026, 4 May 2026, 30 Jul 2026, 21 Oct 2026.

**We do not need any personal information.** Names, phone numbers and house numbers can be removed; road or block level is enough. Any file format is fine, including spreadsheets or scanned logs.

We will use the data only for this coursework. We will not share the raw records, we will credit the Council in our report, and we will publish only maps and summaries that you agree to. We are happy to send you our final results, which may be useful for drainage planning.

If another office holds these records, we would be grateful to be pointed to the right contact.
