## 3. Recommendations for this project

### 3.1 RQ3: can a ranking be defended with about 11 positive cells?

Sourced facts. Ecology method studies put the minimum for any usable model at roughly 14 to 25 presences in real-data cases (van Proosdij et al.), with Wisz et al. reporting no algorithm consistently good under 30 records and recommending exploratory use only. Hernandez et al. found Maxent useful at 5, 10 and 25 occurrences for restricted-range species. Our 11 cells are borderline on the most generous reading, and fewer if co-located landmarks collapse and pumping-only records are set aside. (Inference: the independent presence count may be about 6 to 11 cells.)

Inference, not from a source. With 75 cells and about 11 presences, prevalence is about 0.15, but the unlabelled 64 cells include unreported floods, so any "background" is contaminated. Spatial folds with about 11 positives give folds of 2 to 4 positives each, so performance estimates will be very noisy.

Recommendation:
- Keep the **descriptive evidence map as the primary result**. Offer a positive-only ranking only as an exploratory, clearly labelled secondary output, if MF-50 decides yes. If after de-duplication fewer than about 10 independent presence cells remain, choose "descriptive only". This threshold is my judgement drawing on the sources above, not a published rule.
- Least fragile method family: few covariates (2 to 4, such as elevation, distance to coast, local low-lying position), strongly regularised presence-background (MaxEnt-style, linear/hinge only), or a transparent rank-sum of a few physically justified covariates. Sources support Maxent as the most robust at small n (Hernandez 2006; Wisz 2008), but only for restricted ranges and ecological data. Avoid PU bagging, random forests, neural networks and one-class SVM with many covariates: 11 positives will be overfitted. I found no flood paper showing these work at this sample size (Li 2022 PBLC details on sample size not obtained).
- Metrics to use (all rank-based, no absences needed): recall at k (share of presence cells in the top k or top 20% of cells, compared with the area fraction), leave-one-location-group-out or spatially blocked evaluation (Roberts 2017), and partial ROC (Peterson 2008) or continuous Boyce index (Hirzel 2006). Caution: with 75 cells the Boyce window has very few cells per bin, so report it as indicative and show the P/E curve, not just the number. Boyce at this n is my caveat, not a sourced limit.
- Metrics to avoid: AUC against background/unknowns (prevalence-dependent per van Proosdij; depends on study extent per Lobo 2008; Jiménez-Valverde 2012), accuracy, F1, precision/recall against "non-flood", Brier/log-loss/calibration claims, and POC plots (need prevalence assumptions). Never describe outputs as probabilities.
- Do not rank any cell by raw counts of reports.

### 3.2 RQ4: bias handling for pump sites and landmarks

Sourced: Phillips 2009 supports building the background from locations sharing the same detection bias (target-group background). Aiello-Lammens 2015 and Boria 2014 support thinning clustered records, though evidence on benefit is mixed and no optimal distance exists. Flood-specific sources (Agostini 2024; Esparza 2022) show crowdsourced reports are uneven by population and demographics, and that without extra assumptions unreported cannot be separated from non-events. I found no flood work on news-report or pump-deployment bias specifically ("no source found").

Proposed handling (my inference, to be justified in the report):
1. **Pumping-only records are a separate presence set**, never mixed with observed-inundation records. They indicate where pumps were deployed, which partly reflects where pumps are routinely stationed (for example a hospital and a park). Use them for a sensitivity run, or exclude them from the main model. Do not treat them as flooded unless the text says water was present.
2. **Count co-located landmarks once**: one presence per cell per independent event, and treat cells sharing a landmark group as one location group for evaluation folds.
3. **Target-group-style background**: restrict the unlabelled cells to those that could have been reported (cells containing any named landmark, arterial road or pump station, regardless of whether it was flood-related). This is a loose analogue of Phillips 2009, because no general news-reporting target group exists. If few cells qualify, drop this and use descriptive reporting only.
4. **Bias covariate** (distance to arterial road, landmark density, building/population density) in the background model only, not in the susceptibility score; or run with and without it. Report how rankings change. Population (WorldPop) is modelled, not a census count.
5. Spatial thinning is nearly moot at 200 m cells; one presence per cell already thins. Do not thin further.

### 3.3 What MF-21 must record now (keeps either MF-50 choice open)

Per record: `record_id`, `event_id` (so independent events can be counted), `source_id` and `n_independent_sources`, `report_type` (observed inundation / pumping-only / assistance request / other), `evidence_text_quote`, `mechanism` (rain / coastal / uncertain), `scope` (road, section, junction, landmark, ward, city), `location_group_id` (shared landmark or same site), `cell_id` (or null when scope is wider than a cell), `cell_assignment_method`, `pump_station_routine_flag` (routinely stationed vs event-deployed vs unknown), `arterial_road_flag`, `date_precision` and `date_local` (UTC+5), `rainfall_value` with units and accumulation period (or unknown). Also add `observed_dry` (blank, never defaulted to 0) and `coverage_note`. A reviewer should be able to rebuild "independent presence cells" and "location groups" from these fields without rereading the news.

### 3.4 Other notes

- Recent MaxEnt urban flood papers (Harbin 2025 AUC 0.871; Wuhan multi-scale 2026; Changchun; Ambon) were seen only as search excerpts and not opened. Their reported AUCs are typically against background and should not be treated as validation of the method here. They show use, not adequacy at n about 11.
- Not verified: any claim that the Lamboley and Fourcade 2024 paper, Houston PU flood mask paper, or Detroit hotline paper say what the search summary stated.
