Using AI for Recipe Reviews and User Feedback Analysis Introduction

Your group has been hired as AI engineers and you are tasked with analyzing a dataset containing recipe reviews and user feedback from a popular recipe platform and provide an AI based solution. The dataset includes various features, such as user reputation, comment scores, user interactions, and ratings, allowing for insights into user behavior and recipe performance. Your group will apply AI techniques to identify patterns and predict future outcomes. The assignment will focus on unsupervised learning, supervised learning and neural network based deep learning. You will submit your findings as a comprehensive report with a recorded demonstration.

This is a group work

• Group Size: You are required to work in groups of 2 to 4 members. It is recommended that you form small groups for optimal collaboration.

• Group Confirmation: Before starting the assignment, each group must fill in the Google form provided to confirm their group members. Students must be in groups with their consent. This will be verified later.

Deadline: 23rd August 2027 23:00.

• The completed assignment, including the report, code, and demo link, must be submitted by 23rd August 2025 23:00 to Moodle. Only Moodle submissions are acceptable. Ensure all team members' names and group details are clearly stated in the report.

Objectives

**• Exploratory Analysis and Clustering:**

• Begin by exploring the dataset. Apply an unsupervised learning technique (e.g., clustering) to identify natural groupings or patterns within the data.

• Explain the insights gained from clustering and how it could be used to inform future decision-making.

• Feature Selection and Classification:

• Refine your analysis by selecting a key aspect of the dataset for prediction. Use supervised learning to build a classification model to predict a target feature of your choice.

• Evaluate and interpret the performance of the model. Consider feature importance, accuracy, and its application.

• Regression Analysis for Continuous Prediction:

• Choose another relevant feature and build a regression model to predict a continuous value.

• Assess the model's performance and compare it with other regression techniques.

• Comparison with Neural Networks:

• Compare one of your supervised models (classification or regression) with a neural network model to evaluate different approaches on the same prediction task.

Deliverables

• Visual representation of data, including charts or graphs to support your findings.

• A thorough explanation of the models developed (clustering, classification, regression, and neural networks), along with the evaluation of their performance.

• A conclusion summarizing the insights gained from each model.

**• Code:** Provide the code in a Google Colab notebook and ensure it is shareable. The dataset is provided in Moodle.
